# Game

A cute top-down farming and adventure game built with Godot 4.

## Run

Open this folder in Godot 4 (Import > select `project.godot`) and press F5.

## Controls

- Arrow keys: move
- Space / Enter: talk, pick, plant, harvest, fish, trade, open chests; swings the sword if nothing is in reach
- X / J: swing sword. Shift: dash (brief invulnerability). Q: drink a healing potion
- Sleep at your house door to heal, skip the night and save

## The world

- Farm: crops, a pond, a market stall, small trees to chop for wood, and the road east
- Mossbrook town: fountain plaza (toss a coin for a wish), town hall, bakery, Bram's Builders, Mara's market,
  Wren's pet yard, a pond, a quest board, and a dozen wandering villagers
- Old cave (east end of the road): two floors of monsters, chests, rocks to mine for stone, and the King Slime
- Night raids: monsters attack the farm and town after dark

## Progression

- Levels: kills give XP; each level gives +1 max HP and a full heal
- Pets (level 3+): Wren gives you a dog, bat or ghost that fights beside you and grows with your level
- House building: collect wood (chop trees) and stone (mine cave rocks), then Bram upgrades your farmhouse
  to a Big Cottage and then a Manor (more HP, free potions every morning)
- Seven quests (harvest, mushrooms, fishing, night raiders, slimes, goblins, King Slime)
- Shop: seeds, potions, sword upgrades, heart charms; sells crops, fish and mushrooms
- Outfitter (in the shop): five hats for style and three armor sets that cut damage
- Character creator, day/night cycle, save/load
