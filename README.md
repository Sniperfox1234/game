# Game

A cute top-down farming and adventure game built with Godot 4.

## Run

Open this folder in Godot 4 (Import > select `project.godot`) and press F5.

## Controls

- Arrow keys: move
- Space / Enter: talk, pick, plant, harvest, fish, trade, open chests; swings your weapon if nothing is in reach
- X / J: swing weapon. Shift: dash (brief invulnerability)
- I (or Tab): open the backpack to see everything you own, swap weapon, armor and hat, use potions and food, check quests and change your look
- F: cast fireball (needs mana). M: drink a mana potion. Q: healing potion. E: eat meat or steak
- Sleep at your house door to heal, skip the night and save
- F10 mutes all sound, F11 mutes just the music

## The world

- Farm: crops, a pond, a market stall, small trees to chop for wood, and the road east
- Mossbrook town: fountain plaza (toss a coin for a wish), town hall, bakery, Bram's Builders, Mara's market,
  Wren's pet yard, a pond, a quest board, and a dozen wandering villagers
- Buildings you can walk into: Finn's Bakery (eat in for instant healing and perks), Brann's Armory (hats and
  armor), Gunnar's Butchery (meat and steak that heal you), Tilda's Smithy (swords and axes with fire, ice and
  lightning) and Merlo's Study (fireball spells and mana)
- The Town Hall: a marble hall full of people with stations to use: Mayor Rosa's desk, a quest board that lists every
  job and who gives it, a bank that pays interest, a mirror to change your look, a library bestiary of monsters
  you've beaten, a trophy case of achievements, a town map, and a training dummy
- Fishing: five rarities (common to legendary) that sell for very different prices. React fast on the bite and buy
  better rods from Old Odo, who also keeps a fish book with set rewards
- Dusty's Shooting Gallery: a carnival mini-game with moving targets, bullseyes and golden targets
- Around twenty villagers, including a healer, a postmistress with a daily gift, and a bard who powers you up
- Old cave (east end of the road): floors 1 and 2 are full of regular monsters, chests and rocks to mine for stone;
  floor 3 is the boss chamber with the King Slime, who guards a legendary prize
- Frostfang depths (stairs of ice open below the King's chamber once he falls): a frozen floor of ice slimes, frost
  bats, snow goblins, wraiths and yetis, then the lair of the Frost Titan, with its own legendary prize
- Cooking: gather fish, mushrooms, crops, meat and steak, then cook at the campfire by your farmhouse or in Finn's
  kitchen for long-lasting "well fed" bonuses
- Sound: sound effects for almost everything, music for each area and the boss fights, and day/night ambience.
  All of it is generated by `python3 tools/make_audio.py` into `assets/audio/`
- Night raids: monsters attack the farm and town after dark
- Look: soft, lit visuals. The art is smoothed rather than blocky, the ground is painted by a shader, objects cast
  soft shadows that follow the sun, and each area has its own light, haze, glow and drifting particles
- Travel: Rolf's carriage in Mossbrook (and at every stop) takes you along the king's road for a small fare:
  - Highgarden Castle: a walled royal court with a moat and drawbridge, a keep with a Throne Room (King Aldric),
    Banquet Hall (royal meals) and Royal Armoury (royal weapons and armour), plus a training yard and gardens
  - Sunscar Desert: dunes, an oasis, ruins, Zahra's tent and the pyramid leading into the Sunken Tomb and its Pharaoh
  - Port Saltmere: docks, ships, a lighthouse, ocean fishing with better odds, Salty's outfitters and Smugglers'
    Cove with Captain Blackjaw
  - Mistmere Swamp: boardwalks over dark water, a graveyard, Elda's remedies and the Rotting Bog with Witch Morwen
  - Each dungeon is three floors ending in a boss with its own legendary weapon, armour and hat. Poison and
    slowing statuses show on the HUD, and antidotes from Vera or Elda cure poison

## Progression

- Levels: kills give XP; each level gives +1 max HP and a full heal
- Pets (level 3+): Wren gives you a dog, bat or ghost that fights beside you and grows with your level
- House building: collect wood (chop trees) and stone (mine cave rocks), then Bram upgrades your farmhouse
  to a Big Cottage and then a Manor (more HP, free potions every morning)
- Quests from the town and the far lands: harvests, the caves, the Frost Titan, a royal chain that starts with the
  King's challenge, and a bounty on each of the Pharaoh, Blackjaw and Morwen
- Shop: seeds, potions, sword upgrades, heart charms; sells crops, fish and mushrooms
- Armory: hats for style and three armor sets that cut damage
- Food: press E to eat meat (heals 3) or steak (heals 8)
- Power-ups: orbs drop from monsters (Rage, Swift, Aegis, Heart) and appear mid-boss-fight
- Boss prize: the Slimeblade (a legendary weapon that can heal you) and the sparkling Slime King's Crown
- Character creator, day/night cycle, save/load
